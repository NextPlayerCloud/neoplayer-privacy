# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: March 09, 2026**

TFSC built the **NeoPlayer – Video & Music** app as a **free local media player**.  
This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use **"AS IS"**.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information (if any) when you use our Service.

## Information We Collect and Use

The app primarily operates **locally**:  
It only accesses local media files (videos and music) stored on your device, and this access requires your explicit permission (e.g., storage permissions on Android).  
All playback and processing happen **entirely on your device**.

We use the **Firebase SDK** (provided by Google) for limited crash reporting and performance monitoring (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics). Firebase may collect the following **anonymized data** to help us improve the app:

- Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or fallback identifiers like Instance ID)  
- App usage and crash information (e.g., crash logs, performance metrics)  

**No personally identifiable information** (name, email, phone number, etc.) is collected.  
This data is anonymized and aggregated. It is **not** used for advertising or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Firebase processes this data in accordance with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- No other third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics tools are used beyond Firebase.  
- No user data is sent to our own servers.  
- We do not track location, IP addresses, or any other personal data.

## Permissions

The app requests storage permissions **only** to allow browsing and playing your local media files.  
These permissions are **not** used to collect, upload, or transmit data off your device (except for the limited anonymized data sent to Firebase as described above).

## Log Data

We do not collect detailed log data ourselves.  
Limited crash and performance data may be collected via **Firebase Crashlytics** to diagnose and fix issues.

## Opt-Out Options

You can limit data collection by Firebase:  
- On your Android device, go to **Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization** (this affects Advertising ID usage).  
- Disable app analytics in device settings if available.

## Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13.  
We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.  
If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.  
We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
